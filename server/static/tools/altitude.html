How high did it fly?

Stand a measured distance from the launch pad. When the rocket reaches the top of its flight, sight it with an inclinometer (or a 45° sighting card) and read the angle. Type both in here.

45°At 45°, the height equals how far you walked. If you're 30 steps from the pad and the top of the flight is exactly at 45°, it flew about 30 steps high (plus your eye height).
Spotter 1
Eye height

The angle is measured from your eye, not the ground. About 1.2 m (4 ft) for a grade-school student; hold the inclinometer at eye level.

Spotter 2 (optional)

Put the second spotter on the other side of the pad (or at right angles to the wind). Wind pushes the rocket toward one spotter and away from the other, so the average is closer to the truth.

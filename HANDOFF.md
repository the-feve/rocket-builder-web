### What the designer does now (`server/`, FastAPI + one page with a three.js preview)
- Form → build → 3D preview (exploded, section) → ZIP of print-oriented STLs + `build_notes.txt` + `design.json`.
- Dark theme by default, light toggle (remembered in localStorage).
- **Stability check** (`geometry/rocketgen/stability.py`): Barrowman CP, CG from part centroids + loaded motor + recovery allowance. CG (blue) and CP (red) balls on the model. Under 1.0 cal with the chosen motor the ZIP is withheld (409).
- **Auto-sized fins** (`build_stable`): standard fins grow in span and sweep (not root: a longer root moves the CP forward) until the heaviest motor of the size is stable. M24 1.48x, M24L 1.52x, M29 1.91x; M18/M13 unchanged. Rectangular on M29 hits the 2.5x cap at 0.76 cal and is reported unstable. Custom fins are left as typed.
- **Motor picker** (`geometry/rocketgen/flight.py`, ported from OS_Rocket_Builder `tools/motor-picker.html`; data `geometry/rocketgen/data/web_motors.csv`): every Estes motor for the bay flown in a 1-D sim; checks 5x thrust-to-weight, 15 m/s off the rod, 30 m apogee, and stability with that motor's mass. Recommends the closest safe motor to the target altitude, with its delay. Clicking a row re-checks stability with that motor.
- **Saved designs + share links** (`server/designs.py`): SQLite at `ROCKETGEN_DB`; `/d/<id>` links; an edit key per browser stands in for sign-in. "My rockets" list in localStorage.
- **Site look and iOS/iPad (2026-10-14):** the designer uses fevergeon.com / quiverdesignworks.net's palette (their shared stylesheet: neutral greys, `#0a58ca`/`#6ea8fe` accent, ink-on-paper primary button), follows the system light/dark setting (header toggle pins one) and links back to both sites. iOS: the canvas is absolutely positioned and sized from `#view` (it used to prop the layout open, so the preview didn't follow rotation), `dvh` heights, safe-area insets, pixel ratio capped at 2, render on demand, 16 px fields (no focus zoom), decimal keypad. Phones (≤ 800 px) show the preview above the form with a pinned Build bar and the guide as a full-screen sheet; iPad portrait gets a 320 px form column.
- **Advanced: wall thickness and Body OD offset (2026-10-09):** *Wall thickness* (0.8-3 mm, default 1.5 from the settings CSV) sets the body tubes, nose cone and motor mount (`DesignParams.wall`); the stock tube is the motor bay + 2 walls. *Body OD offset* replaces the absolute Body OD: radial mm over the stock tube (5 = +10 mm diameter), shown live as "Body OD … (stock … + 2 × …)" under the field. The server turns it into `body_od_override`; designs saved with the old override still build and open as the matching offset.
- **Nose insert and cord strap (2026-10-09, after the owner's first prints/preview):** nose shoulder 30% shorter than body joints; the anchor strap is now a straight bar across the bore (wall to wall) with the cord gap behind it and a 45-degree pointed arch underneath (prints without supports). Details in `geometry/report.md` (Deliberate differences).
- **Printer first, tutorial buttons, save names (2026-10-14):** Beginner step 3 is now the printer height and step 4 the rocket height (the report's "try" hints point at the right steps). Saved names (and downloaded design files) get `_<motor>_<fin>_<height>`, e.g. `Zoomer_M18_Trapezoid_340`; the name box shows only the name, and the suffix is stripped and rebuilt on each save. Height snapping and the step-7 buttons: next entry. PR #21 (`claude/height-steps`) was a parallel attempt at the same snapping, superseded by this one.
- **Print orders (2026-10-14):** *Order a printed rocket* under the stability score (stable designs only; must be signed in, so a grown-up account). Form: quantity 1–5, colour, US shipping address, notes, coupon code. `POST /api/orders` rebuilds (or reuses the cached build), then `server/orders.py` saves the order (`orders` table) and its folder `ROCKETGEN_ORDERS_DIR/<batch>/<order id>/` (on Fly `/data/orders`): STLs, `ORDER.txt` batch sheet (customer, address, parts checklist, stability, motor), `order.json`, `design.json`, `build_notes.txt`. Ids `RB-YYYYMMDD-NNN`, batch `BYYYYMMDD` (UTC day). **No payment yet:** a valid coupon (`ROCKETGEN_COUPON_CODES`, comma-separated, case-insensitive) is required and the order is $0; otherwise 402. Limit `ROCKETGEN_ORDERS_PER_DAY` (10) per address. The owner is emailed through Resend (`server/notify.py`: `RESEND_API_KEY`, `ROCKETGEN_NOTIFY_TO`, `ROCKETGEN_MAIL_FROM`; skipped if unset). *My orders* shows the customer's orders and status. **Owner side:** `/admin` (signed in with an email in `ROCKETGEN_ADMIN_EMAILS`) lists orders, sets status (new / printing / shipped / cancelled) and downloads each order's files; `tools/sync_orders.py` (+ `.bat`, `tools/README.md`) copies new/changed order folders to `C:\RocketOrders\<batch>\<order>\` using `ROCKETGEN_SYNC_TOKEN` as a Bearer token. Not yet: payment (Stripe), customer confirmation emails (needs a verified sending domain in Resend), shipping cost, address validation, non-US addresses.
- **Order Rocket Supplies page (2026-10-14):** `/ordersupplies` on this app (rocket.quiverdesignworks.net), rendered by `server/supplies.py` from `server/gear.json` (moved here from fevergeon-site; fevergeon.com/cad/rocket-builder/gear/ now redirects here). 12 categories, 43 Amazon links with Store ID `quiverdesig09-20`, the disclosure "As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases." at the top and bottom, no prices. **Pictures:** each item shows a drawing of its category (`supplies.ICONS`), because Amazon only allows its product images when fetched live through its Creators API (which replaced PA-API; needs 10 qualifying sales in 30 days); an item can instead carry `"photo": "/static/gear/<file>.jpg"`, the owner's own photo. A **First launch starter kit** box sits at the top (`gear.json → starterKit`: five list items, each with a one-line reason). Motor items carry `"motors": ["B4-4", …]`; `/api/options → motor_links` maps each motor to its Amazon packs, and the report's recommended motor gets a *Get B4-4 motors on Amazon* button (single packs first, the variety pack as a second link; if only another delay is listed, it links that and says which delay suits the rocket), with the disclosure next to it. The app links to it as *Order Rocket Supplies* in the header, an *Order rocket supplies* button under the score, and a "Need to get your own supplies…? Order through my link here to support me!" note at the top of the welcome and the full guide. `ROCKETGEN_SUPPLIES_URL` overrides the address.
- **Accounts (2026-10-14, owner chose email + password):** `server/accounts.py`: `users` and `sessions` tables in the designs SQLite file; scrypt password hashes (stdlib); a 30-day session token in the `rb_session` cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure over https), stored as its SHA-256. Endpoints: `POST /api/auth/signup` (needs `age_ok`: 13+ or a parent/guardian, because the site is used by children; COPPA), `/api/auth/login`, `/api/auth/logout`, `GET /api/auth/me`, `GET /api/my/designs`, `POST /api/my/claim` (moves this browser's designs, proved by edit keys, into the account; runs on every sign-in). Designs saved signed in get `owner`; the owner can edit from any browser (`_owned` accepts owner or edit key). Page: *Sign in* in the header (two clicks to sign out), a sign-in / create-account dialog, *My rockets* lists the account's designs when signed in. Sign-up/sign-in share a rate limit (`ROCKETGEN_AUTH_PER_10_MIN`, 20). Not yet: password reset (needs the email service), deleting an account, a privacy policy page (needed now that emails are stored).
- **Printer before height, heights that build (2026-10-14):** Beginner step 3 is now the printer max height and step 4 the rocket height (the printer decides which heights work). `GET /api/heights?motor&print_max_height&fin_shape&nose_shape` returns the overall heights that build as `[first, last]` ranges plus `printer_min`, the lowest printer height that builds any rocket of that motor size (`rocketgen.build.height_ranges` / `min_printer_height`, built on `plan_rocket`, which runs all of `build_rocket`'s checks without solids; a 5 mm scan refined to 1 mm at each edge, under 1 s, `lru_cache`d). A height counts only if it works with plain fins **and** fins auto-sized to the 2.5x limit: grown fins trail further below the tail, so the body is shorter for the same height; checking only plain fins let an M29 at 276 mm through, whose corrective rebuild failed and came out 311 mm. The page snaps the height to the closest one that builds (in the direction it was moved) and a too-short printer up to `printer_min`, with a note under the height field and the allowed range. Sweeps found no gaps in the middle: real builds at every segment-count transition (5 motors × printers 150/200/250) all succeed; what fails is a height too short for the motor bay and fins in the aft segment (Beginner minimums at a 200 mm printer: M13 148–174 mm, M18 201–236, M24 235–281, M24L 260–306, M29 309–364, by fin shape) or a printer too short (M24 < 101 mm, M29 < 133). `_build_to_length` now repeats the corrective rebuild (up to 4 rounds) until the height is within 0.5 mm: a shorter body needs bigger fins, which trail further, so one round left M18 swept at 236 mm coming out 242.7 mm (pre-existing). Checked exact at the new minimums, 16–20 s each. If a corrective rebuild fails (a height typed below the range), it keeps the last build instead of erroring. The step-7 Build bar is no longer sticky during the tutorial, so Back / Finish without building sit in their own row under it (Finish styled as the secondary button).
- **Build retry on a server hiccup (2026-10-14):** a build that was running while a deploy replaced the machine got an empty reply, and the page showed "JSON.parse: unexpected end of data". `postBuild` in `index.html` now retries once after 4 s on an empty, non-JSON or failed reply, then says the server didn't answer (it may have restarted for an update) and to press Build again, instead of telling the user to change their settings.
- **Order buttons after a build (2026-10-14):** *Order it printed for you* (blue) and *Order rocket supplies* (amber) are big side-by-side cards with an icon and a one-line subtitle (`.orderrow` in `index.html`), in the Beginner and full reports; they stack on phones.
- **Opening and tutorial, v2 (2026-10-14):** the form opens locked (greyed, `inert`) with the welcome guide showing and a bar on top (*Start the tutorial* / *Skip tutorial* / *Go to Advanced*); share links and accounts with the *skip tutorial* setting open unlocked. The tutorial now has 7 steps: step 1 is *Make an account* (grown-ups; optional, shows "Signed in as…" once done). Account settings: the header button (signed in) opens an account panel with *Skip the tutorial when I sign in* (`users.prefs`, `POST /api/my/prefs`; also a checkbox on the welcome screen), *My orders* and *Sign out*. The design panel is resizable: drag the handle between it and the 3D view (260 px to 60% of the window, remembered in `localStorage.formWidth`; arrow keys nudge; double-click resets); hidden on phones.
- **Guided tutorial (2026-10-14):** every launch starts in Beginner (the mode is no longer remembered; a saved Advanced design still opens in Advanced). The quick start's *Start designing* runs a tutorial: one step at a time (elements tagged `data-tstep="1".."6"`), everything else greyed out and `inert` (save bar and mode switch too), Back/Next under the step, a sticky progress bar with *Skip tutorial* and *Go to Advanced*. Building on step 6 (or *Finish without building*) ends it and unlocks the whole form; `localStorage.tutorialDone` remembers it, and the quick start then offers *Replay it*. The quick start also has *Skip tutorial* and *Go to Advanced*.
- **Kid-friendly Beginner mode (2026-10-14, owner: aimed at grades 3–7):** Beginner is numbered steps (now: account, motor size, printer height, height, fins, nose, build) with a plain-language "what this does" under each and "More about…" links into the full guide (`#guide-motors`, `#guide-stab`, `#guide-parts`). Motor text lists the real motors for the size from `flight_motors`; height has a slider (150–900 mm) plus the box, with cm / inches / 12-inch-ruler conversions; fins and nose are picture buttons (SVGs drawn in JS; the hidden selects, class `advshow`, are still sent). First visit shows a quick start (`#kidguide`: 4-step process, the cal score explained with a diagram, safety-code note, *Read the full guide*); the Guide button opens it in Beginner and the full guide in Advanced. The Beginner report is a stability-score meter (0–4 cal zones matching `stability.py`: <1 unstable, 1–3 stable, >3 over-stable) with what it means and what to try, the recommended motor with height in m/ft and as a building, its delay and field size, and next steps; the full report is under *Grown-up details*. On phones the report sits right under the 3D view. Save as new / Download file / Open file are Advanced-only. Server unchanged.
- **Beginner / Advanced modes (2026-10-09):** Beginner is the default (remembered in localStorage). It shows motor size, printer max height, **overall height** (nose tip to fin tips / motor cap), nose shape (no fineness) and three fin shapes (swept, delta, trapezoid; default trapezoid; no custom fins). Every other field is sent as the default. The server turns `overall_length` into `body_length` with `rocketgen.build.body_length_for` (exact, using `overall_length()`, which matches `Balance.length`); if auto-sized fins change how far they trail below the tail, `_build_to_length` rebuilds once. Switching modes carries the design over (body length ↔ overall height from the last build); Beginner resets the advanced fields. A saved design with `overall_length` opens in Beginner, any other in Advanced.
- **Build prompts (2026-10-09):** any change shows "Settings changed. Press Build…" above Build (and greys out the ZIP until rebuilt); a progress bar on the 3D view eases toward 95% while building (builds report no progress) with elapsed seconds.
- **User guide (2026-10-10):** before the first build the 3D view shows a guide (`#guide` in `index.html`): how to use the app, Beginner vs Advanced, each report section (stability margin, motor picker columns and pass criteria, parts, build notes, key dimensions), and what OpenRocket is and what to check in it. A **Key terms** glossary (2026-10-10, owner wants it educational) explains stability (CG, CP, calibers, margin, with a diagram, Barrowman), motors (size, the C6-5 code, thrust/impulse, thrust-to-weight), flight (rod speed, apogee, ejection delay, Cd, field size) and recovery/parts. It hides after a build; the **Guide** button reopens it, and the report's "OpenRocket" link jumps to that section. Keep it in step when the UI, the pass criteria (`flight.py`, `stability.py`) or the report change.
- **Design files (2026-10-09):** *Download file* saves `<name>.rocket.json` (`format`, `schemaVersion`, `name`, `mode`, `params`); *Open file* loads it, or the `design.json` from an STL ZIP, as an unsaved design and builds it.
- **Save flow (2026-10-09):** *New rocket* resets to defaults, asks for a name and saves. *Save* overwrites your own design silently. On an unsaved design, a shared one, or *Save as new*, it asks for a name first (copies suggest "<name> (copy)"). A status label shows *Not saved yet* / *Saved · yours to edit* / *Shared design*, and save messages are coloured, with errors caught. The owner found the old flow unclear: saving kept the shared design's name and showed only a small grey line.
- **Hosting guards:** `/healthz`; per-address limits (30 new builds / 10 min, 60 saves / hour); 60 s max wait for the build lock (then 503); failed builds logged with parameters; three.js r160 vendored in `server/static/vendor/` (no CDN).
- **Deploy plumbing:** `Dockerfile` (python:3.12-slim-bookworm), `fly.toml` (app `rocket-fevergeon`, region `sjc` (Fly no longer offers `sea`), shared-cpu-1x 1 GB, suspends when idle, volume `rocket_data` at `/data`), `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (deploys on push to `main`; skips with a notice until `FLY_API_TOKEN` is set).

## How to run locally

```powershell
cd rocket-builder-web
python -m venv .venv; .venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
cd server; pip install -r requirements.txt
uvicorn app:app --reload        # http://127.0.0.1:8000
```
Tests: `python -m pytest -q` in `geometry/` (~1.5 min) and in `server/` (~45 s).

## Print settings

- **Material:** PLA (the masses and flight numbers assume PLA, 1.24 g/cm3). PETG works but is a little heavier.
- **Nozzle and layers:** 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers (0.16 mm for the motor cap if its thread binds).
- **Walls:** 4 perimeters (at least 1.6 mm), so the 1.5 mm tube walls and the fins print solid. Top/bottom 5 layers.
- **Infill:** 20 % gyroid (only the thick bits have any: the tail, the cap, the nose tip).
- **Orientation:** as exported (each STL already rests on z = 0 in its print orientation). Do not rotate.
- **Supports:** none. The overhangs (forward lug underside, cord strap, nose tie bar recess) are 45 degrees or bridged by design.
- **Bed adhesion:** clean PEI or glue stick; brim only on the rod standoff (F).

| Part | Orientation note |
|---|---|
| A | Tip up, shoulder on the bed. The shoulder's bed edge can bulge: sand it before fitting. |
| D | Upright on the tail, as exported; the fins stand vertical, and the forward lug's 45-degree underside and the cord strap in the top need no support. |
| E | Flat face on the bed, as exported. 0.16 mm layers give a smoother thread if it binds. |
| F | Upright, as exported. Tall and thin: add a 3-5 mm brim. |

## Flying it

- **Stability:** 1.15 calibers with the C5 (the heaviest M18 motor) and the default recovery allowance (6.4 g): stable. CG 199.7 mm and CP 224.4 mm from the nose tip; liftoff mass 72.6 g.
- **Recommended motor (app, target 150 m):** **B6-4**, predicted apogee **149 m (489 ft)**, 1.4 cal.
- **Motors that pass the app's checks:** B6-4 (149 m), B4-4 (186 m), C5-3 (316 m), C6-5 (364 m).

| Motor | Apogee | Margin | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1/2A6-2 | 14 m | 1.59 cal | Slow off the rod, Too low for recovery |
| A8-3 | 61 m | 1.55 cal | Slow off the rod |
| B6-4 | 149 m | 1.4 cal | OK |
| B4-4 | 186 m | 1.41 cal | OK |
| C5-3 | 316 m | 1.15 cal | OK |
| C6-5 | 364 m | 1.18 cal | OK |

- **Recovery:** **12 in** parachute (at least 11.3 in): about 4.2 m/s down with 61.3 g.
- **Shock cord:** flat 1/8 in (3 mm) braided elastic, 0.6-0.9 m long (2-3 x the rocket's 300 mm). Round 1/8 in elastic also fits.
- **Field:** at least 160 m on a side with the B6-4 (NAR minimum 60 m).
- Weigh the printed parts and re-run the design in the app (Advanced: mass overrides) before the first flight.

## Assembly order

Follow `docs/assembly/assembly-guide.html` (or the PDF). In short:

1. Check every part: sand the bed-edge lip off the shoulders and the start of the tail thread; no cracks.
2. Dry-fit the joints: A's shoulder slides into the top of D (only the motor cap E is threaded).
3. With the nose off, reach into the top of D (the fin can) and clip the shock cord's lower end onto the strap there with a snap swivel (through the slot behind the bar), or tie it round the bar. Tie its other end to A's tie bar and the parachute on near A.
4. Wadding, then fold in the parachute and cord; fit A.
5. Motor in (nozzle end last), screw on E.
6. At the pad: F clips onto the rod, and the rocket's lugs slide down onto it until the aft lug sits on F.

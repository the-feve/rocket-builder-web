## Print settings

- **Material:** PLA (the masses and flight numbers assume PLA, 1.24 g/cm3). PETG works but is a little heavier.
- **Nozzle and layers:** 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers (0.16 mm for the motor cap if its thread binds).
- **Walls:** 4 perimeters (at least 1.6 mm), so the 1.5 mm tube walls and the fins print solid. Top/bottom 5 layers.
- **Infill:** 20 % gyroid (only the thick bits have any: the tail, the cap, the nose tip).
- **Orientation:** as exported (each STL already rests on z = 0 in its print orientation). Do not rotate.
- **Supports:** none. The overhangs (forward lug underside, anchor strap, nose tie bar recess) are 45 degrees or bridged by design.
- **Bed adhesion:** clean PEI or glue stick; brim only on the rod standoff (F).

| Part | Orientation note |
|---|---|
| A | Tip up, shoulder on the bed. The shoulder's bed edge can bulge: sand it before fitting. |
| B | Upright, as exported (strap prints without supports). Sand the bed edge of the shoulder. |
| D | Upright on the tail, as exported; the fins stand vertical and the forward lug's 45-degree underside needs no support. |
| E | Flat face on the bed, as exported. 0.16 mm layers give a smoother thread if it binds. |

## Flying it

- **Stability:** 1.14 calibers with the D12 (the heaviest M24 motor) and the default recovery allowance (8.2 g): stable. CG 247.4 mm and CP 278.7 mm from the nose tip; liftoff mass 122.3 g.
- **Recommended motor (app, target 150 m):** **C11-5**, predicted apogee **199 m (653 ft)**, 1.36 cal.
- **Motors that pass the app's checks:** C11-5 (199 m), D12-7 (419 m).

| Motor | Apogee | Margin | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| C11-5 | 199 m | 1.36 cal | OK |
| D12-7 | 419 m | 1.14 cal | OK |

- **Recovery:** **15 in** parachute (at least 14.5 in): about 4.4 m/s down with 101.5 g.
- **Shock cord:** flat 1/4 in (6 mm) braided elastic, 0.7-1.1 m long (2-3 x the rocket's 360 mm). Round 1/8 in elastic also fits.
- **Field:** at least 210 m on a side with the C11-5 (NAR minimum 120 m).
- Weigh the printed parts and re-run the design in the app (Advanced: mass overrides) before the first flight.

## Assembly order

Follow `docs/assembly/assembly-guide.html` (or the PDF). In short:

1. Check every part: sand the bed-edge lip off the shoulders and the start of the tail thread; no cracks.
2. Dry-fit the joints: B and D slide together, A's shoulder slides into B (only the motor cap E is threaded).
3. Tie the shock cord behind B's anchor strap, and its other end to A's tie bar; tie the parachute on near A.
4. Wadding, then fold in the parachute and cord; fit A.
5. Motor in (nozzle end last), screw on E.
6. At the pad: F clips onto the rod, and the rocket's lugs slide down onto it until the aft lug sits on F.

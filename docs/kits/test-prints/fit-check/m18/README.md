# M18 fit check

Short slices of the real M18 parts (Rocket Builder's current fits: body joint 0.22 mm, nose 0.27 mm per side, 0.5 mm shoulder chamfer). Print `M18-fit-check.3mf` (one plate, as laid out, no supports) or the STLs.

Each piece has its code engraved on the outside: **18-1** to **18-4**.

- Push **1** (nose bottom) into **2** (top of the body tube, with the cord strap): the nose should slide in by hand and come out with a firm pull.
- Push **4** (shoulder) into **3** (socket): a snug slide that you'd glue.

Heights: nose_bottom 16 mm, top_section 28 mm, joint_socket 18 mm, joint_shoulder 20 mm.

Tube inside diameter in the CAD: 18.40 mm; body shoulder 17.96 mm; nose shoulder 17.86 mm. Measure your prints and send the smallest socket inside diameter and the largest shoulder outside diameter for each pair.

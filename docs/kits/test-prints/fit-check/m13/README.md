# M13 fit check

Short slices of the real M13 parts (Rocket Builder's current fits: body joint 0.19 mm, nose 0.27 mm per side, 0.5 mm shoulder chamfer). Print `M13-fit-check.3mf` (one plate, as laid out, no supports) or the STLs.

Each piece has its code engraved on the outside: **13-1** to **13-4**.

- Push **1** (nose bottom) into **2** (top of the body tube, with the cord strap): the nose should slide in by hand and come out with a firm pull.
- Push **4** (shoulder) into **3** (socket): a snug slide that you'd glue.

Heights: nose_bottom 15 mm, top_section 24 mm, joint_socket 16 mm, joint_shoulder 18 mm.

Tube inside diameter in the CAD: 13.40 mm; body shoulder 13.02 mm; nose shoulder 12.86 mm. Measure your prints and send the smallest socket inside diameter and the largest shoulder outside diameter for each pair.

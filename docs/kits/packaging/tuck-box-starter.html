Steps at a glance
Build it. Fly it.
  1. 1
    Check the fit. Slide ABD together. Snug, not forced.
  2. 2
    Tie the cord. G through the strap in B, the other end to the nose A.
  3. 3
    Add the parachute. Clip the swivel round the cord below the nose knot. Hitch H to it.
  4. 4
    Finish. Glue F beside a fin. Add the decals.
  5. 5
    Motor in. An adult puts in the motor and screws on cap E.
  6. 6
    Pack and launch. Wadding I, parachute, cord, nose on. Count down from 5.
Safety
Follow the NAR Model Rocket Safety Code. Launch outdoors in an open field, away from people, trees and power lines, in winds under 20 mph. Use a launch rod and an electric controller with a safety key. Stand back 5 m (15 ft). Never lean over the rocket. If a motor doesn't fire, pull the safety key and wait 60 seconds before going to the rocket.

Preflight checklist

  • Cord tied at both ends, knots tight
  • Wadding in first, parachute packed loosely
  • Motor cap E screwed on all the way
  • Igniter tip touches the motor's propellant
  • Safety key out until everyone is back
  • Sky clear, wind under 20 mph, no dry grass
